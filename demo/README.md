## Using the subtitles

Drop `subtitles.srt` into any editor (DaVinci Resolve, CapCut, Premiere, iMovie via
conversion) or upload it alongside the video on YouTube. Lines are capped at 48 characters
so two lines stay readable when the video is watched on a phone.

**The cues are written to the script's timings, not to your recording.** After the take,
nudge them to match what you actually said - an out-of-sync subtitle is worse than none.
If a section ran long, shorten the narration rather than the subtitle: the hard 3:00 limit
is a rule, and the timings here already assume you do not rush the six checks at 1:25.

## No burned-in captions

Keep subtitles as a sidecar file or a soft track rather than burning them into the frame,
so the UI stays unobstructed. The judging criterion is that the product is legible; a
caption bar across the match checks works against that.
